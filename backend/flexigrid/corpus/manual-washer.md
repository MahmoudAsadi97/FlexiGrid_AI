## Energy use per program

A 40 °C cotton wash on the W8 uses approximately 0.8 kWh over one hour with the standard 1400 rpm spin. The 60 °C hygiene program uses 1.2 kWh. Most of the electrical energy heats the water: washing at 30 °C instead of 40 °C reduces consumption by roughly a third. Average power during the 40 °C program is about 0.8 kW with a heating peak of 2.0 kW in the first ten minutes.

## Time programming

The end-time function lets the machine finish at a chosen hour up to 24 hours ahead. When an external controller starts the machine, allow a full hour for the program including the spin phase. Do not schedule washing while the household is asleep if the machine is installed near bedrooms; the spin phase reaches 72 dB.

## Installation notes

The machine requires a 230 V, 10 A supply. Transport bolts must be removed before first use. Level the machine with the adjustable feet; an unbalanced machine aborts the spin phase and reports error E4, which extends the cycle and delays a scheduled finish.
