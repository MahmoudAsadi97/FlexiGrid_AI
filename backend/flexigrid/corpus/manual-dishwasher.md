## Programs and energy use

The Eco 50 °C program is the reference program for the EU energy label. It uses approximately 1.0 kWh of electricity and 9.5 litres of water over a two-hour cycle. The Intensive 70 °C program uses about 1.6 kWh in 2 hours 20 minutes. The short 35-minute QuickWash uses 0.8 kWh and is not recommended for heavily soiled loads. Average electrical power during Eco 50 °C is roughly 0.5 kW, with a short heating peak near 1.8 kW in the first 20 minutes.

## Delayed start

A delayed start of 1 to 24 hours can be set with the Delay button or through the home-automation interface. The machine begins the selected program when the delay elapses and reports the remaining time. Delayed start is the recommended way to move the cycle into cheaper night hours when the household has an hourly electricity contract.

## Connection requirements

The appliance connects to a standard 230 V socket on a 16 A circuit. Do not connect the dishwasher and another high-power appliance to the same circuit. The water supply must remain open during a delayed start.

## Maintenance

Clean the filter assembly weekly and run a monthly maintenance wash at 65 °C or higher with an empty machine to prevent grease build-up. A blocked filter increases cycle time and energy use.
