## Electrical characteristics

The HP-6 modulates between 0.6 and 2.2 kW of electrical input. During a steady pre-heating run at mild outdoor temperatures the unit typically draws about 1.4 kW. The backup resistance heater adds 3.0 kW but only engages below −7 °C outdoor temperature or during defrost recovery; scheduled operation should avoid relying on it.

## Pre-heating and thermal buffering

A well-insulated dwelling can be pre-heated one to two degrees above the comfort setpoint during cheap or low-carbon hours and will then coast through the following two to three hours with the compressor off. A typical pre-heat block for this purpose lasts two hours. Running the pre-heat immediately before wake-up gives the best comfort per kilowatt-hour because losses grow with the length of the hold period.

## Quiet mode and scheduling

Night quiet mode caps the fan speed and limits electrical input to 1.6 kW between 22:00 and 06:00 by default. The installer menu allows an external energy-management system to start, stop, and power-limit the unit over Modbus TCP. The minimum compressor run time is 20 minutes; do not schedule blocks shorter than 30 minutes.

## Defrost behaviour

Below 4 °C outdoor temperature with high humidity, the outdoor coil defrosts periodically. Defrost briefly reverses the cycle and can raise input power to 2.0 kW for a few minutes. Leave headroom in any power budget on cold, humid mornings.
