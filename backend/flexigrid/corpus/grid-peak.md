## The evening peak

Belgian system load typically ramps from late afternoon and peaks between 17:00 and 20:00 as households cook, heat, and switch on lighting while commercial demand has not yet fallen away. On cold winter weekdays the evening peak sets the day's maximum. Flexible household load moved out of this window relieves the hours when both the system and local distribution feeders are most loaded.

## Overnight and midday valleys

The deepest demand valley falls between roughly 02:00 and 05:00. A second, growing valley appears around midday on sunny days as solar production suppresses net load. These two windows are the natural destinations for shiftable consumption such as EV charging and water heating.

## Weekday and seasonal variation

Weekend mornings shift about an hour later and peak lower. In summer the evening peak flattens while the midday solar valley deepens. A planner that reads tomorrow's forecasts each evening, rather than assuming a fixed profile, tracks these variations automatically.
