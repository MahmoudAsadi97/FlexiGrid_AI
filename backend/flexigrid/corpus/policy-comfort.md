## Temperature bands

Maintain 19 to 21 °C in the living zones while the home is occupied and awake. Overnight the setback temperature is 17 °C. The bathroom radiator may run 30 minutes before the first shower. Temperatures outside these bands require an explicit override from a resident.

## Wake-up and departure

On weekdays the household wakes at 06:30 and the last person leaves at 08:00. Pre-heating may finish up to 30 minutes before wake-up; heat delivered earlier than 04:00 is mostly lost through the building envelope and should be avoided unless grid conditions strongly favour it.

## Noise and sleep

Appliances audible in the bedrooms — the washing machine spin phase in particular — must not run between 23:00 and 06:00. The dishwasher in the kitchen is acceptable at night with the door zone closed. The heat pump's night quiet mode satisfies the noise requirement.

## Delegation to the planner

The household accepts automatic scheduling of the EV charger, dishwasher, washing machine, and heat-pump pre-heat, provided every deadline stated in a mission is met and the connection-capacity limit is respected. Cooking, lighting, and electronics are never rescheduled.
